Signal decoder

Every meter bar on this site spells something in Morse.

How the bars encode letters

Each column sits in one of three height bands. Judge by eye, or count the lit segments:

What you seeLit segmentsMeans
A tall column, well into the top third11 to 15dash —
A middling column, around a third up4 to 7dot •
A stub at the base1end of a letter

The bars bounce but never leave their band, so the reading holds while they move. A dot at its peak is four segments below a dash at its floor. Stubs hold still.

Read left to right. Two stubs in a row are a word space. The header spells my name. No other field spells the heading above it, so position gives nothing away.

Decode as you read

Tap a button for each column. Or type: full stop for a dot, hyphen for a dash, space for a break, backspace to undo.
