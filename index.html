About

I am a master’s student and researcher at the SecAI Lab at Sungkyunkwan University (SKKU), and a security engineer by background. My work covers both sides of one problem: how AI assistance changes what an attacker can do at scale, and what a defender needs in order to keep pace. Before moving into research, I designed SOC architectures and SIEM ingestion pipelines for regulated environments. The limiting factor there was rarely detection logic. It was alert volume that no team could triage, which is the constraint my research starts from.
