Passive collection

What you sent ahead

You opened a page. Before you read a word of it, your browser had already answered a series of questions nobody asked it. Here are the answers, as this page received them.

Nothing below is stored, logged, or transmitted. There is no analytics script, no cookie, and no localStorage. Every value was computed in your own browser and disappears when you close the tab. Nothing loads from a third party either: the fonts are served from this site rather than from Google. Open the network panel while this page loads and you will see requests for this page's own files and nothing else. The one exception I cannot remove is GitHub, which serves the files and therefore sees your address. That, and why any of this exists, is in the ethics note.

What is missing

One panel is deliberately absent. Your IP address reveals your city and your internet provider, and it arrives before any of the above, in the connection itself. Reading it needs a server, and this site is static files on GitHub Pages, so it has no server to read it with. It is the single most identifying thing you sent, and the only reason it is not on this page is that the hosting cannot reach it.

That is worth sitting with. The most revealing signal here was withheld by an accident of hosting rather than by anyone's restraint.
